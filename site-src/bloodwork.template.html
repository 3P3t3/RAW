The panel

What the panel is

It’s bloodwork, drawn at home. A kit comes to you, you take the sample yourself, and it goes back to the lab in the post.

No prescription to get first. No appointment to sit through. You pay for it once — it isn’t a subscription.

After the panel

What can come after

If you want to go further than the panel, that part is medicine, and it isn’t mine to hand out.

You fill in an intake. A licensed clinician reads it and decides whether a prescription is appropriate for you. They can also decide it isn’t, and say no.

A prescription is refilled every 28 days.
